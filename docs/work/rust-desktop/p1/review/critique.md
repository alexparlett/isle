# Review: critique and consistency

P1 stage 6, the whole-program pass. Each surface spec (`surfaces/`) against
the brief's seven principles and against its structure sheet
(`structure/`), the surfaces against each other, and the specs' token
names against `system/tokens.kdl`. Paths are relative to
`docs/work/rust-desktop/p1/`; `DESIGN.md` is `docs/DESIGN.md`. Findings
only: nothing here has been applied.

## Summary

The specs hold together well. The glass rule, the text-3 rule, the
Reduce Motion and Game Mode forms and the concentric corners are kept
almost everywhere, and every spec has a Reduce Motion and a Game Mode
form. Where the specs drift, a shared part has been drawn twice: the
segmented control, the quiet badge, the close circle, the attention
mark, a capsule bar's radius and the window menu's material. Two token
values disagree with their specs (`size-bar-height`, the annotation
palette). One colour pair fails contrast (white on dark `danger` in the
badge). The accent is used for more than the one thing in four places
(Mission Control's two rings, the tray's attention dot, Settings'
changed dots, Cleaner's basket mark). Against the sheets, most findings
are states, sheets or drag forms the sheet requires and the spec does
not draw; a handful contradict the sheet.

Counts: 185 findings, 21 blocker, 82 fix, 82 polish; 19 questions for the user.
