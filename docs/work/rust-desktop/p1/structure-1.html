Gate 2, batch 1: questions

After revision 1. Each has a recommendation; the reasoning is in the sheets. Settled by revision 1 and no longer asked: OSDs are the island's, the island's click and hover, where privacy marks sit, the status overflow, the Dock's reveal.

Island

  1. Clock: time only, or weekday, date and time? Time only.
  2. Glyphs: only the listed status glyphs, or any Control Centre module pinnable as one? Only the listed ones.
  3. Activities from any program (builds, long jobs)? Only the listed sources for now, revisited early: with no Dock, progress is out of sight until Cmd+Tab.
  4. Queue order: prompt, recording and sharing, call, timer, media. This order.

Menu bar on demand

  1. No window focused: the menus are Files', as Finder's on macOS. Files.
  2. Ctrl+F2 opens the system menu, as on macOS. System menu.
  3. An "always show the menu bar" setting: it would fit in the strip. Not for now.
  4. Menu case: Apple's title case for standard items (Hide Others) or sentence case everywhere (Hide others)? Your call; title case in the sheets now.

No Dock

  1. Favourites: a row of your own apps at the top of Spotlight's Apps grid, the rest alphabetical? Yes, no folders or pages.
  2. Badges at rest: anywhere? No; the notification is the signal.
  3. Default hot corners: bottom left Mission Control, bottom right the Apps grid, so the pointer alone can switch and launch? Yes.

Switcher

  1. Entries: preview cards, not macOS's icon row. Cards.
  2. Second screen: every app, no current-screen filter. Every app.
  3. Apps in the background: all of them, listed last; Q stops one. All.

Window chrome

  1. Option-drag brings the zone grid to the pointer. Yes.
  2. Repeated chords cycle half, two-thirds, third. Yes.
  3. Almost-maximise: none; the centred 60% is the large readable size. None.
  4. Other apps' dialogs attached under their parent when smaller. Yes.
  5. Not responding: closing offers Force quit or Wait. Yes.
  6. Shortcuts-inhibit granted always, shown by an island glyph and a notice. Always.
  7. Bottom edge: no drag zone, as macOS. No zone.

Across sheets

  1. Widgets: one surface, the full-screen dashboard on a chord. Yes.
  2. A windowed game: shell shown and working, without transitions or blur. Yes.
