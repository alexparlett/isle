Gate 2, batch 3: questions

Each has a recommendation; the reasoning is in the sheets. These surfaces are built after Isle replaces v1, so nothing here holds up milestone M1.

Notification Centre and widgets

  1. Where Notification Centre opens: unfolding from the pill under the clock, or a right-edge column as macOS? From the pill: the clock is at top centre, and it makes one shape with Control Centre.
  2. Beside the notifications: the date, month and agenda, no widgets. Yes.
  3. Dashboard: one canvas per screen with finer cells, no pages. One canvas.
  4. Notifications widget: a glance (each app with a count) opening Notification Centre. A glance.
  5. Dashboard chord: v1's Super+A can't carry over (Super is Cmd, and Cmd+A is Select All). Ctrl+Option+Cmd+W proposed. Accept, or name another.

Mission Control and windows

  1. Desktop switch: a short slide with a crossfade rather than a full 3440 px slide. Short slide.
  2. Dropdown terminal: hides when another window takes focus, with a setting to keep it. Hides.
  3. Picture in picture over a full-screen game: hides and keeps playing, returns after. Hides.

Apps

  1. Files: tags: none for now, with a place kept for them. None for now.
  2. Files: undo: a stack per window, as Finder, not v1's one step. A stack.
  3. Files: Connect to Server (⌘K, SMB/NFS): left out unless you reach a NAS or another machine's share day to day. Left out; tell me if you do.
  4. Monitor: GPU as a sixth resource with per-process GPU memory for the 5070 Ti and the iGPU. Yes.
  5. Keychain: revealing a secret asks for your password or fingerprint once on opening and again after idle, as the Passwords app. Yes.
  6. Cleaner in Settings › Storage: a bar by kind and Recommendations that open Cleaner, as macOS. Yes.
