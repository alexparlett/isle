Gate 5, wave A

The specs are in p1/surfaces/. Decided there, for you to confirm or change:

  1. Strip and bar: the island's strip and the revealed bar are both 48, so the bar's titles and the island share a centre line (the same share of the screen as a notched MacBook's bar).
  2. Colour on the island sits in small solid wells (privacy marks, the recording dot), because colours on the translucent pill drop to about 2:1 over a white window.
  3. Menus: the bar's menus and the shell's are glass; menus inside an app's window are on the window's raised surface; menus opened from glass panels are solid, so glass is never on glass. The open title is neutral; the accent is only the selected row.
  4. Isle's mark for the system menu: an island seen from above. The alternative was a filled pill, which reads as a minus.
  5. Traffic lights keep macOS's red, amber and green as the one fixed exception to colour roles; their glyphs are drawn, not Phosphor (too thin at 12).
  6. Zone preview is clear glass with a hairline, never the accent.
  7. Switcher selection is a neutral plate, as macOS, not an accent ring; it appears after 150 ms so a quick tap draws nothing.
  8. Default wallpaper: a drawn backdrop; the shipped set adds four pictures rendered for Isle and a NASA photograph of Earth's night side, each in dark and light.
  9. The softened wallpaper washes toward light grey in light mode, not darker, so the lock's dark text has a light ground.
