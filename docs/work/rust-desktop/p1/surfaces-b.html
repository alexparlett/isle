Gate 5, wave B

The specs are in p1/surfaces/. Decided there, for you to confirm or change:

  1. Banners arrive from the trailing edge; the same card is plain (no glass, smaller corner) inside Notification Centre so glass never sits on glass.
  2. Sliders on glass fill with the text colour, as the island's volume bar; sliders in windows keep the accent.
  3. The lock adds its own wash over the softened wallpaper so its text passes over any picture; a 96-point display time; the wrong-password shake stays (two small decaying cycles), dropped under Reduce motion.
  4. Prompts are one glass frame over a scrim on every screen, so no app can fake one; the action takes the accent only after a 600 ms armed moment.
  5. Force Quit: Cancel is the default; the destructive button never is.
  6. Spotlight has no magnifier, uniform 40-point rows, Finder's selection in the Apps view, and solid menus over its glass.
  7. Markup uses a fixed eight-colour palette, red by default.
  8. Settings: switching panes is a quick crossfade; a found setting is ringed; changed settings marked by a dot.
  9. App conventions: alternating rows only in multi-column tables; empty states one line, no illustration; the toolbar's primary action never the accent.
