Gate 5, wave C

The specs are in p1/surfaces/. Decided there, for you to confirm or change:

  1. Mission Control dims the plain wallpaper under a veil; hot corners show no feedback of their own; a desktop switch is an 80-point slide with a crossfade.
  2. Notification Centre colours calendars from Isle's graph colours, not each feed's own, so none can pass for the accent or a status.
  3. The widgets dashboard draws on the lock's wash; an empty widget shrinks to a one-line strip; nothing staggers in.
  4. The on-screen keyboard is solid rather than glass, so it costs no blur over a game.
  5. Files navigates and switches views instantly; Quick Look grows from the item; a cut item is dimmed and outlined in dashes.
  6. Monitor's graph moves once per sample rather than every frame.
  7. Keychain fetches no site icons (monograms instead), and treats verification codes as secrets.
  8. Cleaner has ten kind colours chosen with a colour-blindness check, always with a symbol and a name; its map zooms from the tile.
  9. Graph colours fixed: the stage 4 series failed a colour-blindness check. The new four (cyan, pink, violet, amber) pass it for every pair, in both appearances, and stay clear of the status colours and the blue accent (system/data-colours.md).
  10. Two kind-colour exceptions: "other" is grey on purpose, and the dark set is a little lighter than the check prefers, because each kind's tile carries a dark symbol that needs a light fill.
