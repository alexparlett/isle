Gate 4: the system

Frost made into tokens, a contrast check and the shared components. The token file and its schema are what P3 reads.

Decided here, for you to confirm or change

  1. Text styles: macOS's at Mac density (body 13, minimum 10), Inter with optical sizes, JetBrains Mono for code.
  2. Contrast policy: every text role 4.5:1 and status marks 3:1 against their own surface, both appearances; on glass, tertiary text is never used; Increase Contrast switches glass to its tinted end, Reduce Transparency to solid.
  3. Accent default: a deeper blue than macOS in dark mode, so white text on it passes; eight accents, each with its own fill, text-on and text forms.
  4. Corners: window 10, panel 18, card 10, control 6, row 5, with the concentric rule.
  5. Motion: five critically damped springs; exits stiffer; Reduce Motion a 120 ms fade.
  6. Separators stay light by default; only under Increase Contrast do borders reach 3:1.
  7. Symbols: Phosphor (MIT): regular for outlines, fill for on, bold under Increase Contrast. Lucide was rejected for having no fill, Material and Fluent for reading as Android and Windows.
  8. App icons: Papirus (Papirus-Dark in dark), keeping each app's own shape; Isle's five apps get icons drawn for them.
  9. Cursor: Bibata Modern Classic at 24, as v1.
  10. Installed apps are themed from these tokens (GTK, libadwaita, Qt, kitty, btop, zathura; system/theming.md), with a 16-colour terminal palette checked for contrast.

Found along the way

  1. Isle serves the appearance settings itself: the GTK portal gives apps light or dark and contrast but not the accent or reduced motion, so Isle's portal backend also takes the Settings portal (an addition to B13).
  2. Qt's file dialog: with v1's GTK platform theme, Qt apps open GTK's own file chooser rather than Isle's panels; P14 tests the alternatives.
