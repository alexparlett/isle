How it works

A new app, Store, in Package Installer 2.0's shape: Discover, Browse, Installed and Updates in a sidebar, packages shown as apps from AppStream. Store's Updates covers Flathub, the AUR and AppImages, each a section with Update All and an Update per app; Update Everything runs those. Settings › Software Update is the system, as on a Mac: CachyOS (its kernel, drivers, Mesa and tools, and the repository apps that update with it) and Isle. Every catalogue is in it (CachyOS, Flathub, the AUR, AppImages), and each is always named where you are looking.

For you to answer

Reply lgtm or name changes. Recommended:

  1. Name: Store, short like Files and Monitor, and "Store…" in the system menu beside System Settings…, each counting its own updates.
  2. Build Updates and Installed first; Discover, Browse and app pages after, in the same packet.
  3. Settings › Software Update is the system: CachyOS and Isle. CachyOS as one card naming the kernel, the graphics driver, Mesa and its tools, and the apps from CachyOS it also updates; one Update Now, since pacman upgrades all or nothing. Isle's card with the commits since your build. The restart owed is said there.
  4. Store's Updates by catalogue: Flathub, the AUR and AppImages, each a section with Update All and an Update per app; Update Everything runs those three. Apps from CachyOS are listed too, saying they update with the system, with a link to Software Update.
  5. Every catalogue, all on: CachyOS, Flathub, the AUR and AppImages. What you are looking at is always named: each catalogue has its own sidebar page saying what it is and who stands behind it; every app and package wears its catalogue's badge; search groups results by catalogue; an app in more than one picks its catalogue on its page. Catalogues… turns one off.
  6. AppImages update from their developers where they embed update information; one that updates itself says so.
  7. Get with updates waiting asks to update and install together.
  8. What an app brings is said twice: under Get on its page ("also installs 14 packages, 532 MB in all"), and in a sheet listing them that Get opens before the password, from anywhere. An app that brings nothing new installs without the sheet.
  9. Remove takes what only it needed (pacman -Rns) after a sheet listing it; an app another package needs is refused, naming it.
  10. Optional extras are offered, never installed for you: "Works better with" on the app page, each with its own Get.
  11. Discover's features are Isle's own list, shipped with Isle; rows come from AppStream categories.
