P1 decided all of this at gates 2 and 5 (p1/structure/mission-control.md, p1/surfaces/mission-control.md); this is the first time most of it has been drawn. Reply lgtm or name what to change. Worth a look in particular:

  1. The spread's arrangement: rows, each window kept near its side of the screen, never larger than real. The alternative is macOS's looser placement, which keeps positions better and packs worse.
  2. The bar always at full size with live miniatures, rather than macOS's names that grow only when the pointer reaches the top.
  3. The tray of minimised and hidden windows along the foot, which macOS does not have (its Dock holds them).
  4. Closing from the tiles: the close badge on hover, W or Delete from the keys.
  5. Show Desktop's slivers: 24 points of each window left at the edges.
  6. Split View's chooser in the free half, and misfits shown dimmed with a reason rather than left out.
  7. Stage Manager's column hidden until the left edge is pushed, unlike macOS's default.
  8. The desktop switch: the short 80-point slide with a crossfade (gate 2's choice), not a full-width slide.
