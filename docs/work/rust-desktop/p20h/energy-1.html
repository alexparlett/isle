How it works

An app that asks to keep the computer awake (Claude's session, a music player, a download) keeps it running: the computer won't sleep, but the screen dims, locks and turns off on its usual timers. Apps you list under Apps that keep the display on get more: while they hold, the screen stays on and the computer stays unlocked. A video player already keeps the display on by itself.
